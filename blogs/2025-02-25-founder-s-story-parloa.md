Malte Kosub and Stefan Ostwald, Co-Founders, Parloa. How Malte Kosub and Stefan Ostwald turned software as a service into service as software — - Parloa, co-founded by CEO Malte Kosub and CPO Stefan Ostwald, is on a mission to revolutionise how businesses interact with their customers using advanced agentic AI technology for customer service. But like many businesses, their mission began with a more prosaic moment: a shared vision sparked from a chance meeting at a conference in Berlin back in 2016.
